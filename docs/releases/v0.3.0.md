Dubnator v0.3.0 makes the Launchpad surface feel more like a live dub sound
system and improves playlist loading workflow.

## Highlights

- Smooth Dub Sound System Wake-Up animation when MIDI controllers connect.
- Faster, smoother Launchpad LED and VU feedback at approximately 40 Hz.
- Samples are now available on the last page of the left / Mix surface.
- Master controls are now available on the fourth page of the right / FX surface.
- Playlist rows now have separate `LOAD` (paused) and `LOAD & PLAY` actions.

## Downloads

- **macOS:** universal DMG for Apple Silicon and Intel
- **Windows:** MSI or setup EXE
- **Linux:** AppImage, DEB or RPM

Full changes: https://github.com/DenshiNingen/dubnator/compare/v0.2.0...v0.3.0
