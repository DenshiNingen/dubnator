Dubnator v0.2.0 turns two Launchpad Mini MK3 controllers into a complete,
performance-ready hardware surface while making the app more reliable offline
and on desktop.

## Highlights

- Complete dual Launchpad Mini MK3 integration with independent left and right
  surfaces, automatic pairing, role swapping and native Tauri MIDI support.
- Sixteen hardware pages cover all 200 performance controls, including mixer,
  isolator, samples, siren, echo, reverb, filter, transport and advanced
  settings.
- Stateful LED feedback with meaningful control colours, live VU meters, peak
  hold, fader positions and synchronized toggle/solo/kill states.
- Exact 0 dB hardware steps for gain, trim, EQ and centered controls, plus
  bidirectional kill/fader behaviour.
- Siren and FX layouts optimized for simultaneous performance: siren controls
  on the left surface and aligned echo/reverb controls on the right.
- Compact interactive Launchpad maps in Help, shown only while Launchpads are
  connected.
- Refactored Studio modules and a hardened offline build with local runtime
  assets, content-versioned caching and desktop-native MIDI.

## Downloads

- **Windows:** MSI or setup EXE
- **Linux:** AppImage, DEB or RPM
